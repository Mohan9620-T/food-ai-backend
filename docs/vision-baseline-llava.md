# LLaVA vision baseline

Captured on 2026-09-02 before the Qwen3-VL migration.

## Environment

- Model: `llava:latest`
- Ollama version: `0.33.2` (meets the Qwen3-VL minimum of `0.12.7`)
- Docker memory limit: 7.518 GiB
- Idle container memory before inference:
  - backend: 89.87 MiB
  - postgres: 49.69 MiB
  - ollama: 42.61 MiB
- Prompt used for every image: `Describe what is visible in this image.`
- Timing: wall-clock time around the existing `ChatVisionService.describe` call

The repository had no committed real-image fixtures. With approval, the first two samples were
read in memory from distinct images persisted in the local chat database. They are intentionally
not committed. The third sample is the existing 2x2 solid-red PNG generated by the pytest fixture.

## Raw results

- Wall-clock time: **109.329 seconds**
- Raw response:

> The image shows a plate with various types of food on a table. There are several pieces of what
> appear to be bread or buns, and next to them, there's a small dish containing a reddish-orange
> condiment, possibly a type of chutney or sauce. The buns are round with a slightly fluffy texture,
> suggesting they might be freshly made. The dish next to the buns is small, which could indicate
> that the condiment is meant to be used sparingly or is part of a larger meal.
>
> In the background, there is a wooden table and what looks like a person sitting behind the table,
> partially visible. The table and the person are not the focus of the image, as they are out of
> focus and not interacting directly with the food.
>
> The setting appears to be a casual dining environment, possibly in a home or a small restaurant.
> The food items are neatly arranged on a wooden plate, suggesting care and attention to
> presentation. The buns and the condiment dish are the main focus of the image, with the person
> and the table serving as context for the food being displayed.

### Generated red PNG fixture

- Wall-clock time: **51.748 seconds**
- Raw response:

> The image is a close-up view of a red surface, which could be a piece of fabric, paper, or a table.
> The color is solid and uniform, and there are no distinguishable patterns, textures, or objects

## Baseline observations

- LLaVA required **51.748–109.329 seconds** per request on this machine.
- The food response remained generic ("bread or buns") rather than identifying a specific dish.
- The second response contains an internal contradiction: it calls the fish a clownfish and then
  identifies it as Dory, which is a blue tang character. This is a clear accuracy failure.
- The synthetic red-image response was appropriately cautious.

These samples and the exact prompt must be reused for the Phase 1-2 comparison.
